# WordPress published state and duplicate scheduling

When authenticated WordPress reports a post as `publish`, Rebound SEO must record that CMS publication even when complete public-page evidence still needs review. Missing featured-image metadata, inline media, canonical agreement, content agreement, indexability, or public reachability must remain visible and must not be mislabeled as fully verified live.

The matching publishing-calendar item becomes `published` only with complete public verification. A CMS-published post with incomplete verification becomes a review item with its public permalink and exact verification reason. It no longer appears past due or remains eligible as an unscheduled topic or approved draft. Calendar labels it `Published — needs review`, keeps the review action visible, and never describes it as verified.

Filtering uses normalized exact keyword identity within the selected website's already tenant-scoped plan, drafts, preferences, and CMS receipt. No CMS request, publication, tenant query, schema, provider credential, or billing behavior is added.
